## Frequently Asked Questions (Dragongrove FAQ)

### How do I increase my dragon habitat capacity without spending premium currency?
To expand habitat capacity naturally, focus on leveling your Sanctuary Tree and unlocking secondary grove sectors via land-clearing quests. Each cleared region grants land plots specifically designated for habitat placement. Furthermore, upgrading your existing enclosures to Tier 2 and Tier 3 increases their per-enclosure capacity from two to four drakes without taking up additional grid tiles.

### What is the most reliable method for breeding rare dual-element dragons?
Breeding higher-rarity dragons requires matching parent drakes with elevated happiness levels (over 90%) and complementary traits. Feeding both parents their favorite high-tier snacks before initiating the breeding ritual noticeably increases the probability of passing down rare traits. Community reports also suggest scheduling breeding cycles during environmental weather bonuses (such as rainstorms for Aqueous offspring) to stack hidden success modifiers.

### How do I cure fatigued or sick dragons after a failed expedition?
Fatigued or wounded dragons must be placed in a Tranquil Spring or assigned to a resting roost equipped with soft bedding. Applying Herbal Salves brewed in the Alchemical Forge cuts recovery time in half. Avoid deploying injured dragons into consecutive missions, as severe exhaustion can temporarily lock their passive production bonuses for up to 48 hours.
