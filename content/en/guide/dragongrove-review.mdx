### How punishing is mount exhaustion during long-distance flights?
If your drake completely depletes its stamina bar over open chasms, it enters a gliding descent rather than plunging fatally. ### Is combat mandatory to progress through the story chapters?
Combat plays a secondary role to exploration and environmental restoration. While wild predators and corrupted entities defend certain ancient roosts, most encounters can be managed using non-lethal deterrent wards, distraction lures, or high-speed aerial evasion tactics.
