## Combat, Flight Dynamics, and Mount Physics

Perhaps the most thrilling reveal in the video was the fluid transition between grounded survival combat and airborne dogfights. Mounted combat has historically suffered in open-world titles, but the mechanics shown here suggest momentum-based aerial physics.

```
Ground Tracking -> Stealth Approach -> Food/Mirror Interaction -> Bonding -> Flight Bond Formed
```

### Key Aerial Flight Mechanics

- **Thermals and Wind Drafts:** Riders can catch warm air columns above geothermal vents to gain altitude without burning their mount's stamina bar.
- **Dive-Bomb Accelerations:** Tilting downward triggers an intense speed boost, allowing players to build kinetic energy for powerful ground-slam strikes.
- **Aerial Maneuvers:** Side-barrel rolls and wing brakes allow riders to dodge ranged projectile attacks from hostile cliffside fauna.

Ground combat looks equally refined. When dismounted, players wield stave weapons, bow-and-arrow armaments, and elemental catalysts, fighting side-by-side with their companions rather than letting them do all the heavy lifting.
