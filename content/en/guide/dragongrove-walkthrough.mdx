If you have searched for "Dragon Grove" and found pages about a Roblox experience, a Steam survival title called *Dragon's Grove*, or the mobile match-3 game *Merge Dragons*, you have landed in the wrong grove. The Dragon Grove covered here is a specific, documented project: a mixed-reality game from Schell Games, built in collaboration with Meta as a client project, and designed exclusively for Mixed Reality on Meta Quest 3 headsets and up. It is free to play, expected in spring 2027, and it is played entirely with native hand tracking — no controller required.

This article sticks to what the developer has actually published. Where something is not documented, it says so rather than guessing.

## What Dragon Grove Actually Is

According to the [official Dragon Grove page on schellgames.com](https://schellgames.com/portfolio/dragon-grove), the game is described as a cozy 3D spatial puzzle experience. Its genre tags are Casual, Puzzle, and Entertainment, and the listed audience is Family and Adult. It runs on Unity and is built for Mixed Reality rather than fully virtual reality.

The core loop combines two activities. First, you solve 3D matching puzzles involving leaves and flowers, using your hands to physically pick up, match, and place pieces in your real room. Second, you care for and bond with magical dragon plants you awaken, petting them, waving to them, and cradling them with your hands.

The official FAQ confirms the game is seated, cozy, and low-stress. That positioning matters: this is not a reflex game or a combat game. It is designed to be played calmly, in short or long sessions, using only your hands.

## Hand Tracking Is the Whole Interface

Dragon Grove has no controller scheme. Native hand tracking is the interface. You match leaves and flowers with your fingers, you pet dragons with your palm, you wave and cradle them. Because the game is mixed reality, your physical space stays visible and becomes the play area.

Practically, that means a few things for anyone planning to play:

- A clear, well-lit room helps hand tracking accuracy. Hand tracking on Quest hardware generally performs better with even lighting and hands visible to the headset cameras.
- Seated play is officially supported, so you do not need to stand or clear a large floor area.
- Because there are no buttons or thumbsticks, there is nothing to remap and no control scheme to learn beyond natural gestures.

Specific gesture lists, calibration steps, or tracking troubleshooting are not documented in the official materials reviewed. You can verify what gestures exist, and how the game handles tracking hiccups, by checking the official page and the in-game tutorial at launch.

## Practical Reasoning for New Players

Because the puzzle side is a matching premise, some general reasoning applies even without measured data. Matching puzzles typically reward scanning the whole board before making a move, since an early match can open or block later ones. In a 3D spatial version with layers, that habit matters more, not less: a piece you match on one layer may change what is reachable on another.

A sensible approach, framed as reasoning rather than proven strategy:

1. Learn the board first. Spend your first moves identifying where plant types cluster and how layers connect.
2. Save boosts. Since every level is completable without power-ups, hoarding boosts for genuinely stuck moments is a reasonable default.
3. Replay for stars. The official page advertises replaying groves to earn more stars, so early groves are not wasted time.
4. Play with dragons between puzzles. Bonding and interaction are core to the experience, not a side activity.
5. Engage with daily streaks if you want steady Coin income, since streaks are an advertised reward system.

None of this guarantees success. It is simply reasoning from how matching puzzles and progression systems generally work, applied to the features Schell Games has confirmed.

## FAQ

**Is Dragon Grove a Roblox game?**
No. It is a mixed-reality game by Schell Games made in collaboration with Meta, built for Meta Quest 3 and up. It is unrelated to any Roblox experience with a similar name.

**Is it the same as the Steam game Dragon's Grove?**
No. The Steam title *Dragon's Grove* from Frozen Flame is a different project entirely. Dragon Grove by Schell Games is a casual mixed-reality puzzle game.

**Do I need controllers?**
No. It is played entirely with native hand tracking. Pet, wave to, and cradle dragons and match leaves and flowers with your hands.

**Is it pay to win?**
The official FAQ and page state it is not pay to win, and that all levels can be completed without power-ups. Coins can be purchased but do not expire, and dragons are unlockable through progression.

**How many levels and dragons are there at launch?**
200+ levels across 30 unique groves, and 18 dragons, all unlockable through progression. Everyone starts with Pip.

**When does it come out and what does it cost?**
It is free to play and expected in spring 2027. Check the [official Schell Games portfolio page](https://schellgames.com/portfolio/dragon-grove) for updates, since the roadmap and patch notes are where further content will be announced.
