### Verdant Hollows (Western Sector)

The Verdant Hollows serve as the natural entry basin for most explorers. Thick with giant moss-grown trunks, winding creeks, and shallow glades, it offers manageable terrain for players mastering basic movement mechanics. Despite its peaceful appearance, dense ground foliage can easily obscure predatory beasts stalking the creek beds. Pay close attention to subtle game trails cut through the fern thickets to avoid triggering ambush nests.

### Wyrmscale Ridge (Northern Highlands)

Ascending toward the north leads straight into Wyrmscale Ridge. This quadrant rises sharply into sheer limestone cliffs buffeted by persistent winds. Navigating this section of the **dragongrove map** demands careful stamina management and precise grappling or gliding maneuvers. Falling from these crags often results in an instant defeat or forces a lengthy trek back up the perimeter ramparts.

### Ashen Roosts (Eastern Canopy)

Characterized by smoke-stained timber and lingering thermal updrafts, the Ashen Roosts represent the historical nesting grounds for ancient drakes. Tree-spanning rope bridges connect massive hollowed trunks, creating intricate aerial pathways. Ground access is heavily restricted here due to petrified brambles, meaning successful traversal relies almost entirely on upper-canopy navigation.

### Sunken Barrows (Southern Underground)

Hidden beneath the southern root systems lies the Sunken Barrows. Unlike the sunlit northern ridges, this sector is an interlocking network of humid cavern chambers and flooded catacombs. Light sources are sparse, making bioluminescent flora and torches necessary to reveal drops, pressure plates, and submerged passageways.
