## Dragon Nurturing and Elemental Care Systems

Dragons within your sanctuary are not static decorations; they are dynamic creatures with individualized biological and environmental preferences. Interacting directly with elemental **dragongrove features** to keep your drakes healthy and content is the foundation of high-yield farming and breeding success.

Each dragon aligns with one or two primal elements: Ignis (Fire), Terra (Earth), Zephyr (Wind), Hydros (Water), and Lumina (Light). Placing a dragon into a mismatched habitat triggers stress, which decreases their daily essence production. Conversely, placing compatible species together generates cooperative aura buffs that elevate the entire grove.

Caretakers must balance three core welfare metrics:

- **Satiation:** Maintained by feeding farm-grown enchanted produce matching the dragon's dietary preference (Carnivorous, Herbivorous, or Mana-based).
- **Enrichment:** Raised through interaction posts, training perches, and elemental toys placed inside the enclosure.
- **Biophilic Harmony:** Dictated by the presence of indigenous flora, appropriate terrain landscaping, and optimal companion species.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Dragongrove Features

### What are the most important dragongrove features for beginners to focus on?
During early progression, prioritize habitat sizing, balanced crop cultivation, and careful mist clearing. Focusing on your primary food supply guarantees your dragons remain fully satiated, unlocking consistent essence yields and reducing breeding turnaround times without draining reserve gold.

### How do adjacency bonuses work across different habitats?
Adjacency bonuses activate when compatible elemental habitats share bordering fence lines or pathway tiles. For example, placing an Ignis habitat adjacent to a Terra habitat creates a volcanic resonance bonus, boosting the resource generation rate of both enclosures by 10%. Incompatible pairings, such as placing Ignis directly next to Hydros, cancel these bonuses and may lower dragon happiness.

### Can dragons lose levels or stats if their happiness drops?
Dragons will not lose earned levels, permanent stats, or acquired traits if their happiness decreases. However, low happiness induces an operational penalty: production yields drop by up to 50%, breeding cool-downs stall, and dragons will refuse to embark on high-tier exploration expeditions until their care needs are met.

### How do seasonal weather changes impact sanctuary management?
Weather systems temporarily shift environmental bonuses throughout your grove. Seasonal rains grant automatic hydration to outdoor agricultural plots and buff Hydros dragon happiness, but they slightly reduce Ignis production. Building canopy covers and elemental shrines allows caretakers to mitigate negative seasonal effects and maintain peak production year-round.
