## Dragon Habitats and Elemental Affinity Mechanics

Every dragon in your sanctuary belongs to one or more elemental alignments, such as Verdant, Pyroclastic, Abyssal, Zephyr, or Celestial. Housing dragons in their favored biome is vital. When a dragon lives in an aligned habitat, its happiness meter rises, directly multiplying its resource generation speed and reducing exhaustion rates.

Placing opposing elements close to one another can destabilize your sanctuary. For example, placing an Abyssal Marsh directly adjacent to a Pyroclastic Crag reduces the emotional stability of both habitats, generating ambient "elemental friction." Based on widespread player experience, isolating conflicting elements using neutral Meadow buffers or Verdant groves prevents these efficiency penalties.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Dragongrove Mechanics

### How do breeding cooldowns impact Dragongrove mechanics over time?
Breeding cooldowns lengthen progressively as a dragon produces more clutches within a short window. This system prevents players from flooding their sanctuary with high-tier eggs without strategic planning. Giving your dragons restorative herbal teas or letting them rest in high-comfort habitats reduces this recovery timer by up to 25%.

### Can you relocate habitats without losing invested upgrade materials?
Yes. The game features an architecture salvage and relocation mode. Moving a habitat retains all tier enhancements, though active dragons within that pen will experience a brief, temporary dip in comfort while adjusting to their new surroundings.

### What is the most effective way to manage elemental friction between habitats?
The cleanest solution is inserting neutral buffer zones. Planting groves of non-elemental Elderwood trees or laying decorative stone plazas between opposing elements absorbs elemental dissonance and keeps dragon happiness at maximum capacity.

### Why did my dragon egg hatch with lower stats than both of its parents?
This typically occurs due to temperature instability during the incubation phase. If the nursery nursery drops below or spikes above the egg's optimal temperature band, the developing hatchling suffers genetic fatigue, resulting in lower base attribute rolls regardless of parental lineage.
