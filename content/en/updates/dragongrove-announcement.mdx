*   **Scale Pigmentation:** Controls base color, metallic sheen, and glowing patterns.
*   **Wing Structure:** Determines gliding speed, maneuverability, and turbulence resistance.
*   **Horn & Crest Morphologies:** Influences intimidating presence and defensive capabilities during encounters.
