If you have seen the phrase "Dragon Grove" online, there is a good chance you have also seen it attached to the wrong thing. Sharing a name with older titles makes searching confusing, so this article sticks to the version that Schell Games has actually announced: a cozy mixed-reality puzzle game built with Meta, playable with your bare hands on Meta Quest 3 headsets and up. Here is what is confirmed, what is not, and how to think about the game before it arrives in spring 2027.

## What Dragon Grove Actually Is

Dragon Grove is an original title from Schell Games, developed as a client project in collaboration with Meta. The [official Dragon Grove page on schellgames.com](https://schellgames.com/portfolio/dragon-grove) lists its genre as Casual, Puzzle, and Entertainment, built in Unity for Mixed Reality, aimed at both family and adult players. It is free to play and expected in spring 2027.

Two things define the experience. First, it is mixed reality rather than fully virtual: the game blends into your real room through the headset. Second, it uses native hand tracking exclusively. There is no controller. You pet dragons, wave at them, cradle them, and match leaves and flowers using your actual hands. The developers describe the tone as seated, cozy, and low-stress, which is a deliberate design choice rather than a limitation.

The game is not a Roblox experience, not the Steam title Dragon's Grove associated with Frozen Flame, and not Merge Dragons. If a page describes any of those, you are on the wrong page.

## Advertised Features and How Progression Works

Schell Games has been unusually clear about the structure, both on the portfolio page and in an official FAQ blog post. The core loop is match-three-style puzzle solving with a spatial twist: new plant types, layered arrangements, and mechanics that use the 3D space in front of you. Completing levels earns Coins.

| Element | What is documented |
|---|---|
| Launch content | 200+ levels across 30 unique groves |
| Dragons | 18 total at launch, all unlockable through progression |
| Starting dragon | Pip, the signature green dragon |
| Currency | Coins from levels, also purchasable; they do not expire |
| Shop items | Dragon hats, tail decorations, auras |
| Optional extras | Dragon toys for fetch and custom perches; dragon boosts as power-ups |

The official page also advertises daily streak rewards, magical charms, tracking friends' journeys, and replaying groves to earn more stars. Dragon toys are explicitly optional and have no gameplay impact. Boosts grant an extra match or ability use, and the developer states that every level can be completed without them. It is not pay to win.

One detail worth repeating because it is often misreported: the official FAQ states the art, character assets, and gameplay contain no AI components.

## Practical Reasoning About the Puzzle Layer

No measured strategy data exists for a game that has not launched, so treat the following as general reasoning from the matching-puzzle premise rather than tested advice. Spatial matching puzzles tend to reward scanning the whole play space before making a move, because a match that opens a lane in 3D may be worth more than the most obvious pair in front of you. If new plant types and layers gate progress, expect each new mechanic to be introduced gently before it is combined with older ones, which is standard for cozy puzzle design. Replaying groves for stars suggests that early levels stay relevant, so thorough clearing beats rushing. Since the developer confirms all levels are completable without boosts, saving Coins for cosmetic hats and auras is a reasonable default for players who care about looks rather than speed.

## FAQ

**When does Dragon Grove come out?**
Spring 2027, per the official page. No specific day has been announced.

**What do I need to play it?**
A Meta Quest 3 headset or newer. It is designed for native hand tracking, so no controller is used.

**Is it free?**
Yes. Coins can be purchased, but the developer states the game is not pay to win.

**How many dragons are there?**
Eighteen at launch, all unlockable through normal progression. Everyone begins with Pip.

**Is Dragon Grove a Roblox or Steam game?**
No. It is a Meta-funded mixed-reality title from Schell Games, unrelated to similarly named games elsewhere.

**Are there AI-generated assets?**
The official FAQ says no, for art, characters, and gameplay.

For anything not covered here, the reliable path is to check the [official Schell Games page](https://schellgames.com/portfolio/dragon-grove) and the in-game menus once you have the headset on. If a claim cannot be traced to those, it is probably speculation.
