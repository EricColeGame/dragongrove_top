## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most important change in this Dragongrove update?
The most impactful change is the complete overhaul of dragon bonding and habitat synergy. Rather than relying on static timers, keepers must now balance environmental purity, customized diets, and elemental biome groupings to achieve optimal companion performance and harvest multipliers.

### How does the Dragongrove update affect existing dragon enclosures?
Existing enclosures retain their general layout, but their efficiency ratings will now reflect environmental synergy rules. If you placed conflicting elemental types side by side, you may notice reduced happiness and minor soil degradation until you separate them or introduce neutral buffer monuments.

### Are wild expeditions harder after the recent patch?
Yes, expeditions now emphasize elemental counters and environmental resistances over brute force. Bringing the proper hazard mitigation gear and matching dragon affinities to the zone's threat profile is essential for securing top-tier crafting components without exhausting your team.

### How do I cure habitat blight introduced in the new update?
Habitat blight can be resolved by deploying purified water trenches, planting restorative cleansing herbs like Sunwort, and utilizing wind drakes to clear stagnant atmospheric haze across affected plots.
